# 3. Retail Sales Analysis

## Goal
Practise loading, checking, cleaning, grouping, and charting a larger dataset.

## Data
50,000 retail transactions. A few regional blank  values were addressed in the cleaning process.

## Run
`python analyse_sales.py`

The script reports total sales, sales by product and region, monthly sales, and saves CSV summaries plus two charts in `outputs/`.

## Questions to explore
- Which product has the highest sales value?
- Which region has the highest sales value after missing regions are labelled `Unknown`?
- Do monthly sales change over time?

## Limitations
This dataset may reflect a possible real life situation but a more roburst dataset will give a more accurate prediction
